# AI Ready School — Homepage Redesign

## Audit: Top 5 UX/Design Problems

### 1. Hero section lacks a scannable value proposition
The current headline "A Complete AI Ecosystem for K-12 Schools" is generic — every edtech company claims completeness. The subtext is a 50+ word paragraph that a busy principal won't read. There's no visual hierarchy to pull out the key differentiator (5 products, on-premise, India-focused).

### 2. Product sections are visually monotonous and create scroll fatigue
All five products (Cypher, Morpheus, Zion, NEO, Matrix) use an identical alternating image-text layout. By the third product, the user is pattern-blind. There's no way to compare or scan products at a glance — you must scroll through all five sequentially.

### 3. Social proof stats are broken/placeholder ("0+" projects, "0%" satisfaction)
The trust section — arguably the most important element for a skeptical school principal — displays placeholder zeros. This actively undermines credibility.

### 4. The page has no visual rhythm or section differentiation
Every section uses the same white background with no color, texture, or spacing variation. No visual anchoring to help users understand where they are on a very long page.

### 5. Navigation categories don't match the user's mental model
Labels like "Philosophy" and "Our AI Platform" are company-centric, not user-centric. A school principal thinks "What does this do for my school?" — not "What's your philosophy?"

## What I Prioritized
1. **Hero**: Clear, scannable value proposition with trust badges above the fold
2. **Navigation**: Simplified to 4 items with descriptive product dropdown
3. **Products**: Tabbed interface replacing 5 identical scroll-sections
4. **Social proof**: Fixed stats, testimonial cards, school logos
5. **CTA**: High-contrast dark section with dual CTAs

## Design Decisions
- **Colors**: Deep navy + warm coral (trustworthy, not generic AI purple)
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (headings) + DM Sans (body)
- **Interaction**: Tabbed product explorer reduces scroll fatigue
